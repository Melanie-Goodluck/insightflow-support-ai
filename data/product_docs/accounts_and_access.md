# Accounts and access

## Inviting team members

Administrators can invite a team member from Settings, Team, Invite Member.
The invitation link expires after 72 hours. A new invitation can be sent after
the old one expires.

## Roles

Administrators manage users, billing, data sources, and all dashboards. Editors
can create dashboards and refresh data but cannot manage billing or team
members. Viewers can only open dashboards shared with them.

## Sign-in problems

After five unsuccessful sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes.
A user can also select Forgot Password to request a password-reset link. The
reset link expires after 30 minutes.
